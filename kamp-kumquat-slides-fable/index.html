Kamp Kumquat Safety Orientation

Fable's deck. Same brief, other model: see Astra's deck.

Designed and tested to WCAG 2.2 AA and Section 508 accessibility standards. Every slide image has a full text description, and the viewer works by keyboard (arrow keys) and touch.

A seven-slide, illustrated orientation for a fictional summer camp. Director Ned runs the briefing while his well-meaning counterpart, Klutzy Karl, makes every mistake so the campers can spot and fix it.

Download PDF Ned's cue card

    Ned's One-Page Cue Card

    Built from the same brief as Astra's deck. Fable turned each topic into a find-the-mistakes picture: six numbered klutzes per scene, a running klutz count, and a matching rule for every mistake. It also delivered Ned's one-page cue card, the whole presenter script on a single sheet.

    Kamp Kumquat, its staff, and its campers are fictional. Slides shown as rendered images; text substitutes a similar typeface for the deck's original font.
