The beer garden

5,000 square feet of outside. One gigantic BBQ.

Patio orders tagged BBQ come off the big grill out back. Three separate grates, three sets of tools, zero crossover — meat, veggie, and vegan never share a surface.

Meat

The hot end of the grill. Burgers, brats, wings, and whatever the pit's doing this week.

Grate 1 · its own tongs & trays

Veggie

Vegan
