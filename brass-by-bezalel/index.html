The Season’s Four

We are four artisans and one forge, working brass the old way — drawn, raised, pierced, and planished by hand, sealed in beeswax, and released one piece at a time. Nothing here is twice.

About our Artisans

Four Pairs of Hands

This house is named for Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur — the first maker called by name in scripture, filled with the spirit “in wisdom, and in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship.” We keep a smaller ambition: four benches, one forge, few pieces, and no two alike.

“To devise cunning works, to work in gold, and in silver, and in brass … to work in all manner of workmanship.” Exodus XXXI · 4–5

Contact Us

Write, Call, or Come to the Court

Whether this is your first hour among our cases or a returning one — thank you. You have looked closely at slow work, and that is a kindness to the hands that made it. Most of what is sold in the world is stamped out by the thousand; you chose to spend your attention on pieces that exist exactly once, and to learn how they came to be.

Every work in this house is begun and finished by a single artisan, and each is happiest meeting its wearer across the bench. If a piece has caught you, write to us, telephone, or better still — take an hour and come sit at the forge.
