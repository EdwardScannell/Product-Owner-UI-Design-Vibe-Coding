Ordering ahead

Two ways to order

Same day: walk in or curbside

Everything on the shelf is ready to go. Call from the lot and we will bring your order out to the car.

Special orders: by 12pm, two days ahead

Large quantities, event orders, or a loaf we are not baking today. Place the order by noon two days before you need it.
